Areas first-time buyers look at

In Antioch, the side of town you pick changes your monthly bill. Here is how it splits.

Newer homes, bigger lots

South of Lone Tree Way

Homes here were built in the 1990s and 2000s. You get three and four bedrooms and bigger lots. Stores and parks are close, and so is Black Diamond Mines. Many homes here have a Mello-Roos tax.

Ranch homes near the river

North Antioch

North Antioch is near the river and the old downtown. You find ranch homes from the middle of the 1900s, and most sit on one level. Many have no Mello-Roos tax. Older homes near downtown need a close look before you offer.

Compare the full monthly cost, not just the price. Ask for the tax bill on any home you like.

What a weekend can look like when you live in Antioch.

Black Diamond Mines Regional Preserve

Black Diamond Mines covers about 8,500 acres of hills just south of Antioch, with about 65 miles of trails. From the 1860s to about 1900, five mining towns worked the coal here, including Somersville. Sand was mined underground from the 1920s until 1949. The Greathouse Visitor Center sits inside an old underground chamber, and on weekends you can book a short walk into a coal mine. Take the Somersville Road exit from Highway 4. Check the East Bay Regional Park District site for hours and parking fees.

Check these before you make an offer

The Mello-Roos tax

It is an extra tax on newer neighborhoods that pays for roads, parks and schools. It can add $200 to $450 a month. Your lender counts it against you, and $400 a month can cut your loan by about $50,000. Many homes south of Lone Tree Way have it. Read the tax bill before you make an offer.

What FHA checks on the house

FHA needs just 3.5% down, but the appraiser checks the house too. Peeling paint, a missing handrail or an old roof can get flagged, and the seller may need to fix them first. Older homes near downtown need a close look before you offer.

A lower rate for the first two years

A 2-1 buydown lowers your rate by 2% in year one and 1% in year two. The seller pays for it as a credit. In year three your rate goes back up, so make sure you can afford the full payment.

Antioch questions answered fast

Can you get from Antioch to San Francisco without driving?

Yes. BART runs from Antioch station to Oakland and San Francisco, so you can read or work the whole way. Highway 4 is slow at rush hour. Antioch works best if you go into the office a couple of days a week. Ride it once before you decide.

What is Mello-Roos in Antioch?

Mello-Roos is an extra tax on newer neighborhoods. It pays for roads, parks and schools, and it can add $200 to $450 a month. Many homes south of Lone Tree Way have it, and older homes in north Antioch often do not. Read the tax bill before you offer.

North or south Antioch for a first home?

South of Lone Tree Way, homes are newer, with three and four bedrooms and bigger lots, but many have Mello-Roos. North Antioch has one-level ranch homes near the river and downtown, and many have no Mello-Roos. Compare the full monthly cost, not just the price.

What is there to do near Antioch?

Black Diamond Mines Regional Preserve sits just south of town, with about 65 miles of trails and a coal mine you can walk into on weekend tours. It was once the site of five coal mining towns. North Antioch's old downtown sits along the river.

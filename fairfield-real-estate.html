Life in Fairfield

Getting to San Francisco
Capitol Corridor trainIt stops at Fairfield-Vacaville and Suisun-Fairfield, with Wi-Fi and food on board. At Richmond you can switch to BART, which takes you into San Francisco. The train also stops in Berkeley and Emeryville.
On foot
Texas StreetDowntown has restaurants, coffee shops, bakeries and shops around Texas and Webster streets, with the historic courthouse close by.
Things to do
Market and festivalEvery Thursday evening from spring through fall, Texas Street holds the Downtown Fairfield Farmers Market. Downtown also hosts the Fairfield Tomato and Vine Festival.

Areas first-time buyers look at

Fairfield spreads across more than 40 square miles. Here is how the main areas differ.

Bungalows near Texas Street

Downtown and Central Fairfield

A few blocks off Texas Street, on streets like Kentucky Street, you find 1940s and 1950s bungalows and mid-century ranch homes on level lots.

Close to I-80 and I-680

Cordelia

Cordelia sits where I-80, I-680 and Highway 12 meet. Homes are mostly from the late 1990s and 2000s. It helps if you work in Walnut Creek.

Foothills next to Napa County

Green Valley

Green Valley sits in the western foothills, with a golf course, vineyards and quiet roads.

Homes around the golf course

Paradise Valley and Rolling Hills

Northwest Fairfield wraps around Paradise Valley Golf Course, with homes from the 1990s and 2000s and trails close by.

Near Travis Air Force Base

East Fairfield

East Fairfield has established subdivisions with wide streets, close to Travis Air Force Base.

In newer parts of Cordelia and Paradise Valley, ask about special taxes like Mello-Roos before you make an offer.

A weekend in Fairfield

What a weekend can look like when you live in Fairfield.

Downtown Fairfield Farmers Market

Every Thursday evening from spring through fall, Texas Street holds the farmers market.

Fairfield Tomato and Vine Festival

Downtown hosts the festival each year. About 25,000 people came in 2024, as reported by ABC10.

Rockville Hills Regional Park

Rockville Hills covers more than 600 acres of grassland and oak woodland, with more than 30 miles of trails for hiking and mountain biking. You need a pass from the entrance to visit, and the park is open sunrise to sunset.

The historic courthouse

The old Solano County Courthouse anchors Texas Street and keeps downtown busy during the week.

Check these before you make an offer

Buying a brand new home

Some builders in Fairfield offer a lower rate at the start or pay part of your closing costs. There is often a catch: you may have to use their lender, so compare that loan with one other lender. Bring your own agent on the first visit, because the sales office works for the builder. Read the builder contract before you sign.

Special taxes in newer areas

In newer subdivisions like parts of Cordelia and Paradise Valley, check for special assessments or Mello-Roos. They are added to your property tax bill and your lender counts them against you.

Fairfield housing programs

Help that works in Fairfield and every other city

  • FHA loan: 3.5% down with a credit score of 580 or higher.
  • VA loan: no down payment for eligible veterans and service members, if the price is not more than the appraised value.
  • CalHFA MyHome: up to 3.5% of the price for your down payment or closing costs, with no monthly payment.
  • CalHFA Dream For All: up to 20% of the price for first-generation buyers, when a round is open.
  • A seller credit: the seller can pay part of your closing costs. See the limits for each loan.

Fairfield questions answered fast

Can you commute from Fairfield without driving I-80?

Yes. The Capitol Corridor train stops at Fairfield-Vacaville and Suisun-Fairfield, with Wi-Fi and food on board. At Richmond you can switch to BART into San Francisco. The ride is longer than a clear drive, so it works best if you go in two or three days a week.

Should a first-time buyer buy a new build in Fairfield?

It can work. Some builders offer a lower starting rate or pay part of your closing costs, and everything in the house is new. But you may have to use their lender, so compare one other loan, bring your own agent and read the builder contract before you sign.

Fairfield or Vallejo for a first home?

Start with where you work. Vallejo has a ferry to San Francisco. Fairfield has the Capitol Corridor train and Cordelia's access to I-80 and I-680 toward Walnut Creek. Fairfield has more homes built after 1990, and Vallejo has more older homes near the water.

What is downtown Fairfield like?

Downtown centers on Texas and Webster streets, with restaurants, coffee shops, bakeries and the historic courthouse. Texas Street holds a farmers market every Thursday evening from spring through fall, and downtown hosts the Fairfield Tomato and Vine Festival.

Is there down payment help for first-time buyers in Fairfield?

Fairfield's city programs are on hold until further notice. Fairfield buyers can still use CalHFA MyHome, which can cover up to 3.5% of the price, an FHA loan with 3.5% down, or a VA loan with nothing down. A seller credit can also pay closing costs.

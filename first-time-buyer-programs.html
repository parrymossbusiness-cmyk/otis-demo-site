First-time home buyer programs in the East Bay

CalHFA, FHA, VA and local down payment help for Oakland, Berkeley, Richmond, Antioch, Vallejo and Fairfield, with what is open now.

The short answer

First-time buyers in the East Bay can use three main types of help. An FHA loan needs 3.5% down if your credit score is 580 or higher. A VA loan needs no down payment for eligible veterans and service members. CalHFA's MyHome program can add up to 3.5% of the price for your down payment or closing costs, with no monthly payment. Some programs, like CalHFA Dream For All and Alameda County's AC Boost, open only in rounds. Check that a round is open before you plan around one. Loans with a low down payment

These loans work in all 6 cities. Many buyers think they need 20% down. Most first-time buyers put down far less.

Available

FHA loan

U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development (HUD) | All 6 cities

You can put 3.5% down if your credit score is 580 or higher. FHA also lets you buy 2 to 4 units if you live in one of them.

Available through most lenders.

HUD on FHA credit scores
Available

VA loan

U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs | All 6 cities

You need no down payment if the price is not more than the appraised value. There is no monthly mortgage insurance. You may pay a one-time VA funding fee. You must live in the home.

Available to eligible veterans, service members and some surviving spouses.

VA purchase loans

Some conventional loans also allow 3% down for first-time buyers. Ask your lender which loan fits you.

State help from CalHFA

CalHFA is California's housing finance agency. Its programs work in every city on this page and have income limits.

Available

CalHFA MyHome Assistance

California Housing Finance Agency (CalHFA) | All 6 cities

A second loan for your down payment, your closing costs or both. It can be up to 3.5% of the price with a CalHFA FHA loan, or 3% with a CalHFA conventional loan. You make no monthly payment on it. Income limits apply.

Listed as active. Ask a CalHFA-approved lender if funds are open.

CalHFA MyHome
Opens in rounds

CalHFA Dream For All

California Housing Finance Agency (CalHFA) | All 6 cities

A loan of up to 20% of the price, with a cap of $150,000, for the down payment or closing costs. At least one buyer must be a first-generation buyer. You pay it back when you sell or refinance, plus a share of the home's gain in value.

The last round closed on March 16, 2026. Watch CalHFA for the next one.

How to start

  1. Take a HUD-approved homebuyer class. Many programs require one, and it helps you with every other step.
  2. Talk to a lender who works with CalHFA programs. Ask which programs fit your income and credit.
  3. Check the status of any local program on its official page before you shop.
  4. Plan to ask the seller for a credit toward closing costs. See how much each loan allows.
  5. Check the hidden costs in the city you pick, like transfer taxes and Mello-Roos. See the hidden costs.

Program questions answered fast

What first-time home buyer programs can I use in the East Bay?

First-time buyers in the East Bay can use three main types of help. An FHA loan needs 3.5% down if your credit score is 580 or higher. A VA loan needs no down payment for eligible veterans and service members. CalHFA's MyHome program can add up to 3.5% of the price for your down payment or closing costs, with no monthly payment. Some programs, like CalHFA Dream For All and Alameda County's AC Boost, open only in rounds. Check that a round is open before you plan around one. Is CalHFA Dream For All open right now?

No. The last round closed on March 16, 2026. Dream For All opens in rounds, so watch the CalHFA site for the next one. It can lend up to 20% of the price, with a cap of $150,000, and at least one buyer must be a first-generation buyer.

Can I use a VA loan to buy in the East Bay?

Yes. A VA loan works in Oakland, Berkeley, Richmond, Antioch, Vallejo and Fairfield. You need no down payment if the price is not more than the appraised value, and there is no monthly mortgage insurance. You must be eligible for a VA Certificate of Eligibility and live in the home.

Can I combine a down payment program with a seller credit?

Often, yes. A down payment program can cover part of your down payment, and a seller credit can pay closing costs. Your loan type sets the most a seller can give. Ask your lender to confirm the two can be used together on your loan.

Who counts as a first-time home buyer?

Most programs count you as a first-time buyer if you have not owned your main home in the past three years. Each program sets its own rule, so read the rules for the one you want.

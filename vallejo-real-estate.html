Buying your first home in Vallejo

A ferry from downtown, homes from the early 1900s and trails by the water. Here is what first-time buyers should know about Vallejo.

Life in Vallejo

Getting to San Francisco
Ferry, about 60 minutesThe ferry leaves from downtown Vallejo and lands at the Ferry Building in San Francisco. There is no bridge and no traffic. You sit down and ride.
On foot
Downtown to the ferryYou can walk to the ferry from the old downtown. From St. Vincent's Hill, you can walk to the waterfront. Georgia Street has coffee, shops and galleries in old brick buildings.
Things to do
Trails by the waterGlen Cove has trails along the Carquinez Strait. On Saturday mornings, the Vallejo Farmers Market sets up on Georgia Street.

Areas first-time buyers look at

Vallejo is not one place. Each area has a different look and a different ride to work.

Homes from the early 1900s

Downtown and St. Vincent's Hill

Victorian, Edwardian and Craftsman homes from the late 1800s and early 1900s, within walking distance of the ferry and the waterfront.

By the Carquinez Strait

Glen Cove

Glen Cove sits along the water on the south side of town. Homes were built in the 1980s and 1990s, many streets are cul-de-sacs, and trails run along the strait.

A golf course community

Hiddenbrooke

Hiddenbrooke is a golf course community off I-80, in the hills toward Fairfield. The homes are newer, with open space around them.

Mid-century ranch homes

East Vallejo

East Vallejo has established ranch-style homes, quiet cul-de-sacs and quick access to I-80.

The old naval shipyard

Mare Island

Mare Island is the former naval shipyard across the causeway from downtown. It has taprooms, studios and open shoreline, and the city has plans for more homes there over time.

Walk all three of Downtown, Glen Cove and Hiddenbrooke before you choose. Each one has a different price and a different look.

A weekend in Vallejo

What a weekend can look like when you live in Vallejo.

Vallejo Farmers Market

On Saturday mornings, the Vallejo Farmers Market sets up on Georgia Street in the historic downtown.

Mare Island Shoreline Heritage Preserve

The preserve covers about 215 acres on Mare Island. A paved trail about a mile long climbs past the Navy's oldest Pacific cemetery, from 1858, to views of San Pablo Bay, the Carquinez Strait and Mt. Diablo. It is open on weekends and holidays, so check the hours before you go.

Glen Cove trails

Trails run along the Carquinez Strait from Glen Cove, with views of ships moving through the strait.

Check these before you make an offer

Ask for a seller credit

Homes often sit longer in Vallejo than in Oakland. After about 21 days on the market, many sellers will talk. A credit at closing can pay your loan fees, appraisal and title costs, so your savings stay in the bank. Your loan type sets how big a credit can be, so ask your lender early.

A loan that pays for repairs

Vallejo has Victorian and Craftsman homes that need work. An FHA 203(k) loan pays for the house and the repairs in one loan, with 3.5% down. It takes more paperwork and an approved contractor, so get a contractor's bid before you offer and give yourself extra time to close.

Vallejo questions answered fast

How long is the ferry from Vallejo to San Francisco?

The ferry from downtown Vallejo to the Ferry Building in San Francisco takes about 60 minutes. There is no bridge and no traffic. You can walk to the ferry from the old downtown and from St. Vincent's Hill. Ride it once before you buy.

Can a first-time buyer fix up an old Vallejo house?

Yes, with the right loan. An FHA 203(k) loan pays for the house and the repairs in one loan, with 3.5% down. It needs an approved contractor and more paperwork, so get a contractor's bid before you offer and plan extra time to close.

Do Vallejo sellers pay closing costs?

Some do. Homes often sit longer in Vallejo than in Oakland, and after about 21 days many sellers will talk. A credit at closing can pay your loan fees, appraisal and title costs. Your loan type sets the limit, so ask your lender early.

Which areas of Vallejo should first-time buyers see?

Start with three. Downtown and St. Vincent's Hill have homes from the early 1900s near the ferry. Glen Cove has 1980s and 1990s homes by the Carquinez Strait. Hiddenbrooke is a newer golf course community toward Fairfield. Walk all three before you choose.

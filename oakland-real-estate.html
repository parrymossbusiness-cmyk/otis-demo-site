Life in Oakland

Getting to San Francisco
BART, buses and the ferryFrom Rockridge station, BART is about 20 minutes to downtown San Francisco. From Grand Lake and the Laurel, Transbay buses run to the city. From Jack London Square, the ferry ride to the Ferry Building takes about 20 to 25 minutes.
On foot
College, Grand and LakeshoreRockridge has restaurants, cafes and bookshops along College Avenue. Market Hall is across from the BART station. Grand Lake has two main streets, Grand Avenue and Lakeshore Avenue.
Things to do
Lake MerrittA 3.4 mile path circles Lake Merritt. You can rent a kayak at the Lake Merritt Boating Center or see a film at the Grand Lake Theater.

Areas first-time buyers look at

Oakland is a big city, and each area has its own homes and its own ride to work. These are areas Otis walks with first-time buyers.

Near BART

Rockridge

Rockridge sits around College Avenue, with shops and cafes a short walk from the BART station.

Condos and walks by the lake

Grand Lake and Lake Merritt

Buildings around the lake put you close to the 3.4 mile path, Grand Avenue and Lakeshore Avenue. Many condos here have an HOA fee, so add it to your monthly number.

Lofts by the water

Jack London Square

Old warehouses on 2nd, 3rd and 4th streets have been turned into lofts with brick walls and big windows. The ferry dock and the Bay Trail are close.

Newer homes on the water

Brooklyn Basin

Brooklyn Basin has newer buildings along the waterfront, with paths that connect to Jack London Square. Otis has a full tour of it.

Houses with yards

Maxwell Park and Millsmont

Maxwell Park has 1920s bungalows with yards. Millsmont sits up in the foothills, and some homes have bay views. I-580 is close, so downtown Oakland is a short drive.

Close to MacArthur BART

Longfellow

Longfellow sits next to Emeryville and North Oakland. MacArthur BART is close, so the trip to San Francisco is short.

Prices change from street to street in Oakland. Check recent sales near any home before you make an offer.

A weekend in Oakland

What a Saturday or Sunday can look like when you live here.

Grand Lake Farmers Market

The Grand Lake Farmers Market runs on Saturday mornings, a short walk from the lake and the Grand Lake Theater.

Jack London Square on a Sunday

On Sunday mornings, the plaza at Jack London Square fills with the farmers market. You can rent a kayak or paddleboard on the docks.

The Bay Trail

From Jack London Square, the paved Bay Trail runs along the water toward Brooklyn Basin. You can walk, run or ride without getting in the car.

Lake Merritt Boating Center

You can rent a kayak or a small boat and see the city from the middle of the lake.

Check these before you make an offer

The city transfer tax

Oakland adds a 1.5% tax when most homes sell. The buyer and the seller often split it. Some first-time buyers qualify for a lower city rate based on income. Check the rules with the City of Oakland.

The sewer pipe under the yard

The pipe from the house to the street must pass a test when a home sells. EBMUD runs that program in Oakland. A new pipe can cost $5,000 to $12,000, so Otis writes it into your offer. Check the address with EBMUD before you offer.

Do this weekRide BART or the ferry once at rush hour before you choose an area. Then ask for the tax and sewer details on any home you like.

Home Access Program

Housing Trust Silicon Valley | Homes in Alameda and Contra Costa counties

A loan of up to 40% of the price, with a cap of $200,000. Household income must be at or below 80% of the area median. You make no monthly payment for 30 years, and the loan charges 3% simple interest.

The program site does not say if it is taking applications. Check before you plan around it.

Alameda County | People who live in, work in or were displaced from Alameda County

A down payment loan for first-time buyers that you repay with a share of the home's gain in value.

AC Boost

Help that works in Oakland and every other city

  • FHA loan: 3.5% down with a credit score of 580 or higher.
  • VA loan: no down payment for eligible veterans and service members, if the price is not more than the appraised value.
  • CalHFA MyHome: up to 3.5% of the price for your down payment or closing costs, with no monthly payment.
  • CalHFA Dream For All: up to 20% of the price for first-generation buyers, when a round is open.
  • A seller credit: the seller can pay part of your closing costs. See the limits for each loan.

Oakland questions answered fast

How long is the ride from Oakland to San Francisco?

From Rockridge, BART takes about 20 minutes to downtown San Francisco. From Jack London Square, the ferry takes about 20 to 25 minutes to the Ferry Building. Transbay buses also run from Grand Lake and the Laurel. Ride it once at rush hour before you choose an area.

What costs surprise first-time buyers in Oakland?

Two costs catch buyers. Oakland adds a 1.5% transfer tax when most homes sell, and the buyer and seller often split it. The sewer pipe must also pass an EBMUD test, and a new pipe can cost $5,000 to $12,000. Check both before you offer.

Can first-time buyers pay less transfer tax in Oakland?

Some first-time buyers qualify for a lower city transfer tax rate. It depends on your income. You can also ask the seller for a credit that covers the tax and your closing fees. Check the rules with the City of Oakland before you write an offer.

Where do first-time buyers look for houses in Oakland?

Otis often starts first-time buyers in Maxwell Park, Millsmont and Longfellow. Maxwell Park has 1920s bungalows with yards. Millsmont sits in the foothills near I-580. Longfellow is close to MacArthur BART. Homes in these areas can need some work, so plan for an inspection.

Is there down payment help for first-time buyers in Oakland?

Oakland's CalHome loan has been out of funds since August 1, 2024. Alameda County's AC Boost is also closed. Housing Trust Silicon Valley's Home Access Program covers Oakland, so check if it is open. Statewide, CalHFA MyHome can cover up to 3.5% of the price, an FHA loan needs 3.5% down and a VA loan can need nothing down.

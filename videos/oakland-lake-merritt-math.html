Lake Merritt condo or Uptown rent

Otis runs the full monthly cost of a condo near Lake Merritt, from the loan to the HOA fee, and compares it to renting.

Updated October 19, 2026 by Otis White Jr.

What Otis says

If you rent in Uptown, run this math before you buy your first condo. Say a condo near Lake Merritt costs $550,000. And you put 5% down. The loan payment is about $3,200 a month. Property tax and insurance add about $700. Then there's the HOA fee. Buildings around the lake often charge $500 to $850. Add it up, and you're near $4,500 a month. Now here's the part renters don't hear. Rent in Uptown can be lower on day one. But rent never comes back to you. Every loan payment pays down what you owe. Buying makes more sense if you stay 4 years or more. Ask a lender to run your own numbers. Share this with a friend who rents in Oakland.

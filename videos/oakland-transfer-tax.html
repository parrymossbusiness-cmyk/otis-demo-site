Oakland's home sale tax

Otis walks through Oakland's city transfer tax, who usually pays it, and two ways first-time buyers can lower what they owe.

Updated October 19, 2026 by Otis White Jr.

What Otis says

This Oakland tax surprises a lot of first-time buyers at closing. On a $700,000 home, it's $10,500. It's called the city transfer tax. The rate is 1.5% on most Oakland homes. That covers homes from $300,000 to $2 million. The buyer and the seller often split it. So your half is about $5,250. But this is where first-time buyers can have an edge. Some qualify for a lower city rate. It depends on your income. You can also ask the seller for a credit. That credit can cover this tax and your closing fees. Ask about both before you write an offer. And check the rules with the City of Oakland. Comment GUIDE and I'll send you my free First-Time Buyers Guide.

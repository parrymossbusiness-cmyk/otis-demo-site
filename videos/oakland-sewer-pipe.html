The $10,000 sewer pipe in Oakland

Otis explains the sewer pipe test in Oakland and other EBMUD cities, what a new pipe can cost, and why it goes in your offer.

Updated October 19, 2026 by Otis White Jr.

What Otis says

First-time buyers in Oakland, this pipe can cost you $10,000 after you close. It's the sewer pipe under the yard. It's called a private sewer lateral. When a home sells here, that pipe has to pass a test. EBMUD runs the program in Oakland. Alameda, Albany, Emeryville and Piedmont too. So what if it fails? A new pipe can cost $5,000 to $12,000. Here's the part that catches buyers. The seller can pay a deposit and get 180 more days. Then the repair can land on you. That's why we write it into your offer. The seller fixes it, or gives you a credit. Check the address with EBMUD before you make an offer. Comment GUIDE. I'll send you my free First-Time Buyers Guide.

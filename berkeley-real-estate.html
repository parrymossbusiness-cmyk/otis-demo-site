Life in Berkeley

Getting to San Francisco
BART and Transbay busesDowntown Berkeley, North Berkeley and Ashby all have BART stations. AC Transit Transbay buses run to the Salesforce Transit Center in San Francisco. Interstate 80 runs along the west side of town.
On foot
Shops close to homeMany buildings in central and downtown Berkeley are a short walk to shops, BART and the UC Berkeley campus. North Shattuck, College Avenue and 4th Street each have their own row of shops.
Things to do
Food streets and parksNorth Shattuck is home to Chez Panisse and the Cheese Board Collective, which has been there since 1967. The Berkeley Rose Garden and Indian Rock are in North Berkeley.

Condos near BART

Central and downtown Berkeley

Condo buildings near BART and the campus. Many are a short walk to shops. This is one way into Berkeley that does not start with a house.

A different way to own

TICs in older buildings

A TIC, or tenancy in common, means you own a share of a small building and the right to live in your unit. You see them in older Berkeley buildings. A TIC needs a special loan, often 10% to 15% down, so talk to a TIC lender first.

Berkeley homes are often listed below what they sell for. Look at recent sold homes, not the list price, before you make an offer.

Check these before you make an offer

The transfer tax and the earthquake refund

Berkeley charges a 1.5% transfer tax on most homes, and buyers and sellers often split it. If you make the home safer in an earthquake, like bolting it to the foundation, the city can refund up to one third of the tax, up to $4,000. You have one year after the sale, and the work has to qualify. Check the rules with the City of Berkeley.

Fire insurance in the hills

Homes near Grizzly Peak sit in high fire zones, and many insurers will not write a policy there. You may need the California FAIR Plan with a second policy on top. Near San Pablo Avenue, most homes are outside those zones. Check the fire map for any address and get a quote before you remove your contingencies.

Down payment help in Berkeley

We did not find a City of Berkeley down payment program open in October 2026. The city has pointed buyers to CalHFA Dream For All. These are the local programs to watch.

Check first

Home Access Program

Housing Trust Silicon Valley | Homes in Alameda and Contra Costa counties

A loan of up to 40% of the price, with a cap of $200,000. Household income must be at or below 80% of the area median. You make no monthly payment for 30 years, and the loan charges 3% simple interest.

The program site does not say if it is taking applications. Check before you plan around it.

AC Boost

Help that works in Berkeley and every other city

  • FHA loan: 3.5% down with a credit score of 580 or higher.
  • VA loan: no down payment for eligible veterans and service members, if the price is not more than the appraised value.
  • CalHFA MyHome: up to 3.5% of the price for your down payment or closing costs, with no monthly payment.

Berkeley questions answered fast

Can first-time buyers afford Berkeley?

Many first-time buyers get into Berkeley through condos or TICs instead of houses. Condos near downtown BART are a short walk to shops and the campus. TICs in older West Berkeley buildings often cost less than condos, but they need a special loan. Ask a lender which one fits you.

What is a TIC in Berkeley?

A TIC, or tenancy in common, means you own a share of a whole building and the right to live in your unit. They often cost less than a condo. You need a special TIC loan, often 10% to 15% down, and a lawyer should read the TIC agreement.

Does Berkeley refund part of the transfer tax?

Yes. If you make the home safer in an earthquake, Berkeley can refund up to one third of the transfer tax, up to $4,000. You have one year after the sale, and the work has to qualify. Keep every permit and receipt, and check the rules with the City of Berkeley.

How do you get from Berkeley to San Francisco?

Berkeley has three BART stations: Downtown Berkeley, North Berkeley and Ashby. AC Transit Transbay buses run to the Salesforce Transit Center. Interstate 80 runs along the west side. Try your commute at rush hour before you choose an area.

Is there down payment help for first-time buyers in Berkeley?

We did not find a City of Berkeley program open in October 2026. Berkeley buyers can use CalHFA MyHome, which can cover up to 3.5% of the price, and CalHFA Dream For All when a round opens. Housing Trust Silicon Valley's Home Access Program covers Alameda County, so check if it is open.
